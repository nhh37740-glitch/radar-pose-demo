Dataset attribution, license and citations

This page replays a contiguous 240-frame excerpt (original sequence frames 0–239) of recorded sequence 2019-01-15-13-06-37, spanning 59.73 seconds of capture. It contains paired radar and stereo images and saved per-frame pose estimates. 不在浏览器中运行模型推理。 The browser runs no model and performs no live inference.

Dataset material and the included excerpt of reference/derived pose records are attributed to the Oxford Radar RobotCar Dataset and the Oxford RobotCar Dataset and are shared under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). The official Oxford Radar RobotCar site and the official Oxford RobotCar privacy and license page describe dataset terms. Changes: a contiguous interval was selected, the paired sensor images are distributed as web-ready JPEGs, and the pose rows were excerpted to the same interval. This license applies to the included dataset material and excerpted records; it does not grant rights to unrelated project code. This public demonstration is provided for non-commercial academic use.

The datasets include street imagery. The Oxford Robotics Institute asks people with privacy concerns about content or inclusion in the dataset to contact the dataset maintainers; see the official privacy and license notice.

Dataset attribution

Oxford Robotics Institute, University of Oxford: Oxford Radar RobotCar Dataset and Oxford RobotCar Dataset. The source data has been excerpted and packaged with paired image timestamps; no new sensor capture is performed by this demo.
